Opening Remarks

Learning Outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  1. Introduce your manuscript, its stage, and its target outlet to the group.
  2. State the intended contribution of your manuscript in one sentence.

Contribution Sentence

Contribution Clarity

If you cannot state your contribution in one sentence, you do not have it yet.

We contribute by [mechanism or boundary condition] explaining [X → Y] for [literature or audience].

Thatcher (2025) includes contribution clarity in the diagnostics box [Appendix E]. The template forces three decisions: what the paper adds (a mechanism or a boundary condition), which relationship it explains, and for whom the explanation matters. You write a first version at the start of the course and revise it at the end of the first day, after the desk-reject simulation and the assumption hunt.
